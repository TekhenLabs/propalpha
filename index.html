What we do

PropAlpha makes cash offers to property owners. We buy as-is: no repairs, no cleaning, no agent commission, and a closing date that suits you. We are buyers, not real estate agents, and we do not list homes for sale.

We buy in the Chicago area, New Jersey, the Cleveland area, Houston, Dallas and San Antonio.

Why we called you

Public property records show you own a property that may fit what we buy. We call to ask whether you would consider an offer. There is no obligation, and a "no" is always fine.

Don't want our calls?

Tell the caller "please stop calling" on any call. We record it right away, and we will not call that number again.
