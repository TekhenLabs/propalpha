Frequently Asked Questions

Do you buy all types of land in Jacksonville?
Yes. We buy vacant residential lots, raw acreage, agricultural land, infill lots, and undeveloped parcels of all sizes in the Jacksonville metro area.
Do I need a survey before selling my land?
No. We work with existing county records, plat maps, and GIS data. If a survey is needed for closing, we cover the cost.
My land is tax-delinquent. Can I still sell?
Absolutely. We specialize in purchasing tax-delinquent land. We'll pay the back taxes as part of the closing and you keep the remaining proceeds.
I inherited land I've never seen. Can you still buy it?
Yes. Many of our sellers inherited land and have never visited the property. We handle all research and paperwork, and can purchase sight-unseen by you.
My land is in a flood zone. Will you still buy it?
Yes. We buy land in all flood zones, including Zone A and AE. Flood zone status affects our offer price but doesn't disqualify your property.
